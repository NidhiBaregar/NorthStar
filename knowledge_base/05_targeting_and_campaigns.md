# Targeting and Campaign Strategy

## Targeting

Targeting should consider:
- response probability
- Financial Value Proxy
- segment
- contact fatigue
- channel
- campaign objective
- outreach cost

STP is the primary framework for targeting.

High predicted response can justify priority treatment.

High value combined with sufficient response opportunity can justify personalization.

High fatigue combined with weak response supports reducing or pausing outreach.

Low response should not automatically mean exclusion when customer value is high. Consider improving the offer, message or targeting.

## Campaign Metrics

Response Rate = Responses / Customers Contacted

Cost per Response = Campaign Budget / Responses

High-Fatigue Contacts = Customers Contacted × High-Fatigue Percentage

Contact frequency bands:
- 1–2
- 3–4
- 5–6
- 7+

## Campaign Diagnosis

If fatigue rises by more than 10 percentage points while response improvement is below 3 percentage points:
→ tighten targeting before increasing outreach.

If response improves by at least 3 percentage points and high fatigue remains at or below 30%:
→ additional outreach may be justified selectively.

Otherwise:
→ focus on targeting and message quality.
