# Customer Segments

## Segment 1 — Low-Engagement, Low-Value

Cluster 0.

Approximate profile:
- 13,315 customers
- average age 39.9
- average balance 1,040
- average campaign contacts 2.69
- almost no previous contacts
- response rate 5%

Strategy:
- low-cost nurture
- relevant broad messaging
- selective escalation

Avoid expensive one-to-one sales effort at scale and repeated outreach without evidence of improvement.

## Segment 2 — Moderate-Value, Low-Response

Cluster 1.

Approximate profile:
- 13,881 customers
- average age 43.0
- average balance 1,236
- average campaign contacts 3.06
- almost no previous contacts
- response rate 11%

Strategy:
- improve offer relevance
- improve messaging
- test targeting
- avoid simply increasing contact volume

## Segment 3 — High-Potential, Engaged

Cluster 2.

Approximate profile:
- 7,880 customers
- average age 41.0
- average balance 1,514
- average campaign contacts 2.07
- average previous contacts 3.24
- response rate 23%

Approximately 19% show previous campaign success in the segment profile.

Strategy:
- targeted sales follow-up
- relevant personalization
- selective priority treatment
- monitor contact frequency

Segment 3 is the strongest response opportunity.

## Segment 4 — High-Value, Untapped

Cluster 3.

Approximate profile:
- 10,135 customers
- average age 39.4
- average balance 1,840
- average campaign contacts 3.00
- very little previous contact
- response rate 12%

The segment has a high share of management jobs, tertiary education and cellular contact.

Strategy:
- personalized high-value proposition
- carefully selected outreach
- relevance rather than simply more contacts

Segment 4 is the strongest high-value opportunity.

## Important Distinction

Segment 3 = strongest response opportunity.

Segment 4 = strongest financial-value opportunity.

Neither should automatically be called the universally "best" segment.
