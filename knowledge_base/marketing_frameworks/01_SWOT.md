# SWOT Analysis

SWOT:
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Opportunities
- Threats

Strengths = internal characteristics that create advantage.
Weaknesses = internal limitations.
Opportunities = external or situational conditions that can be leveraged.
Threats = external or situational conditions that can negatively affect outcomes.

Use SWOT at customer, segment, campaign or broader strategy level.

For customer-level SWOT, use actual customer evidence. Do not invent motivations, competitors, economic conditions or preferences.
