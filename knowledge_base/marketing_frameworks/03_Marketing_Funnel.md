# Marketing Funnel

Common stages:
1. Awareness
2. Interest
3. Consideration
4. Conversion
5. Retention

Use the funnel to match marketing intervention to customer progression.

Low engagement may require awareness or nurture.

Strong predicted response may justify conversion-focused outreach.

Repeated contact without response may indicate a need to change the message, offer or targeting.

The dataset does not directly observe every funnel stage.
